# Northstar Labs — Customer Support Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Department scope:** Customer Support
**Access scope:** All authenticated users

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how the Customer Support department handles external user inquiries, technical issues, and escalations.

## 2. Service Standards

### 2.1 Response Times

| Priority | First Response | Resolution Target |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| Critical (system down) | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| High (major impact) | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| Medium (partial impact) | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| Low (minor or question) | 1 business day | 5 business days |

### 2.2 Communication

- All communication is professional, clear, and jargon-free.
- Customers receive a ticket reference number for every business request.
- Status updates are provided at least once per business day for open tickets.

## 3. Issue Classification

Support agents classify every issue into one of the following categories:

1. **Question** — informational inquiry, no action required.
2. **Technical issue** — requires diagnosis and possible IT escalation.
3. **Complaint** — dissatisfaction with product or service.
4. **Refund or return request** — financial in nature, forwarded to Finance.
5. **Account access issue** — forwarded to IT.

## 4. Escalation Rules

- Issues unresolved after two troubleshooting attempts are escalated to IT.
- Complaints involving financial impact are escalated to Finance.
- Any issue involving a security incident is escalated to IT immediately and flagged as critical.

## 5. Refund and Return Handling

Customer Support may explain refund, return, and warranty policies but does not execute refunds or modify financial records. Refund requests are forwarded to Finance with a structured summary.

## 6. Knowledge Base

Agents use the company knowledge base (RAG) to answer questions. If no relevant document exists, the agent escalates to a senior agent or department manager.

## 7. Privacy

Customer personal data is handled according to the data protection policy. Agents must not request sensitive information (passwords, full payment card numbers) via chat or email.
