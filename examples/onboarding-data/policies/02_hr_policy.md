# Northstar Labs — Human Resources Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Department scope:** Human Resources
**Access scope:** All authenticated users

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs employee lifecycle processes including leave, onboarding, benefits, and internal rules.

## 2. Leave Policy

### 2.1 Annual Leave

- Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave must be requested at least 5 business days in advance.
- A maximum of 15 days may be carried over to the next year.
- Minimum staffing constraints apply: at least 50% of a department must remain present.

### 2.2 Sick Leave

- Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
- Medical certificates are required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days.
- Sick leave does not carry over.

### 2.3 Parental Leave

- Maternity leave: 16 weeks paid.
- Paternity leave: 4 weeks paid.
- Additional unpaid parental leave up to 12 months may be arranged with HR.

### 2.4 Leave Approval

1. Employee submits a leave request through the platform.
2. HR verifies remaining balance and staffing constraints.
3. Department manager approves or rejects.
4. HR updates leave records after approval.

## 3. Onboarding

New employees complete the following checklist within their first week:

- Sign employment contract.
- Complete tax and payroll forms.
- Receive IT access and equipment (coordinated with IT).
- Attend orientation session.
- Meet with direct manager for role expectations.

## 4. Benefits

### 4.1 Health Insurance

All full-time employees and their dependents are eligible for the company health insurance plan. Enrollment is automatic on the start date.

### 4.2 Retirement

Employees may contribute up to 10% of salary to the retirement plan. The company matches up to 5%.

### 4.3 Wellness

Employees receive a monthly wellness stipend of $100 for gym, mental health, or wellness-related expenses.

## 5. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require HR and executive approval.

## 6. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year (January and July). Reviews include self-assessment, peer feedback, and manager evaluation.

## 7. Termination

Termination requires HR approval and documentation. Notice periods are 4 weeks for standard employees and 8 weeks for directors.
