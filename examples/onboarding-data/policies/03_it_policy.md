# Northstar Labs — Information Technology Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Department scope:** Information Technology
**Access scope:** All authenticated users

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines IT access management, asset management, software provisioning, and security standards.

## 2. Access Management

### 2.1 Access Requests

- All system access is requested through the platform.
- Requests require department manager approval.
- IT provisions access within 1 business day of approval.
- Access is granted on a least-privilege basis.

### 2.2 Access Review

- IT reviews access rights quarterly.
- Unused accounts are deactivated after 30 days of inactivity.
- Departing employees lose all access within 24 hours of termination.

### 2.3 Authentication

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and numbers.
- Passwords are rotated every 90 days.

## 3. Asset Management

### 3.1 Equipment Assignment

- Laptops are assigned to employees on their start date.
- Monitors, keyboards, and peripherals are provided as needed.
- All assets are tagged and recorded in the asset registry.

### 3.2 Asset Return

- Employees return all company assets on their last working day.
- Lost or damaged equipment is assessed on a case-by-case basis.

## 4. Software Catalog

### 4.1 Approved Software

Only software from the approved catalog may be installed on company devices. The catalog includes:

- Productivity: Microsoft 365, Slack, Zoom
- Development: VS Code, Git, Docker
- Design: Figma
- Security: CrowdStrike, 1Password

### 4.2 Software Requests

Requests for software not in the catalog require IT and Finance approval. IT evaluates security and compatibility; Finance evaluates cost.

## 5. Security

### 5.1 Incident Response

Security incidents are reported to IT immediately. IT follows the incident response plan:

1. Identify and contain the incident.
2. Assess impact.
3. Eradicate the threat.
4. Recover systems.
5. Conduct a post-incident review.

### 5.2 Data Protection

- Sensitive data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Customer data is accessed only by authorized personnel.
- Data exports require manager approval.

## 6. Network

- VPN is required for remote access.
- Guest Wi-Fi is isolated from the corporate network.
- Personal devices on the corporate network require IT registration.

## 7. Backups

- Critical systems are backed up daily.
- Backups are retained for 30 days.
- Backup restoration is tested monthly.
