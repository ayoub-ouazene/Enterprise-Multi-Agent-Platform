# Northstar Labs — Code of Conduct (Shared Policy)

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Department scope:** Shared (all departments)
**Access scope:** All authenticated users

---

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical and professional standards expected of every employee, contractor, and representative of Northstar Labs. It applies across all departments and supplements any department-specific policies.

## 2. Core Principles

### 2.1 Integrity

All employees act honestly and transparently. We do not misrepresent facts, data, or outcomes in any internal or external communication.

### 2.2 Respect

Every individual is treated with dignity. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind is prohibited and will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### 2.3 Accountability

Employees take ownership of their work. Mistakes are reported promptly, and corrective actions are taken without concealment.

### 2.4 Confidentiality

Company information, client data, and intellectual property are protected. Employees must not share confidential information outside authorized channels.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or family interests that could conflict with their duties. Disclosure is made to the employee's department manager and the HR department.

## 4. Use of Company Resources

Company equipment, software, and network access are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties or violate security policies.

## 5. Reporting Violations

Violations of this Code should be reported to the HR department or through the confidential reporting channel. Retaliation against anyone reporting a violation in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## 6. Disciplinary Action

Violations are addressed proportionally. Depending on severity, consequences may include verbal warnings, written warnings, suspension, or termination. The HR department manages the disciplinary process.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed annually by HR and executive leadership. The latest active version is the authoritative source.
