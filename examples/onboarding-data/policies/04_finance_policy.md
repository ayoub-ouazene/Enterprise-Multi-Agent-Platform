# Northstar Labs — Finance Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Department scope:** Finance
**Access scope:** All authenticated users

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs budget management, expense approvals, refunds, and financial decision-making.

## 2. Budget Management

### 2.1 Annual Budgets

- Each department receives an annual budget approved by Finance and executive leadership.
- Budgets are reviewed quarterly.
- Departments may not exceed their annual budget without Finance approval.

### 2.2 Budget Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Personnel | Salaries, benefits, training |
| Operations | Software, services, subscriptions |
| Capital | Equipment, infrastructure |
| Travel | Business travel and accommodation |
| Miscellaneous | Other approved expenses |

### 2.3 Budget Transfers

Transfers between categories up to 10% of the category total may be approved by the department manager. Transfers above 10% require Finance approval.

## 3. Expense Approvals

### 3.1 Approval Thresholds

| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| Up to $500 | Department manager |
| $500 – $5,000 | Department manager + Finance |
| $5,000 – $50,000 | Finance Director |
| Above $50,000 | Executive leadership |

### 3.2 Expense Submission

- Expenses are submitted through the platform within 30 days of incurrence.
- Receipts are required for all expenses above $25.
- Expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days of approval.

## 4. Refunds and Returns

### 4.1 Customer Refunds

- Refunds are processed within 5 business days of Finance approval.
- Full refunds are issued for returns within 30 days of purchase.
- Partial refunds are evaluated case-by-case for returns after 30 days.

### 4.2 Refund Approval

- Refunds up to $100: Customer Support may recommend; Finance auto-approves.
- Refunds $100 – $1,000: Finance approval required.
- Refunds above $1,000: Finance Director approval required.

## 5. Invoicing

- Invoices are issued within 3 business days of service delivery.
- Payment terms are net 30 days unless otherwise agreed.
- Overdue invoices are escalated at 15, 30, and 60 days.

## 6. Financial Reporting

- Monthly financial reports are generated by the 5th of each month.
- Quarterly reports are reviewed by executive leadership.
- Annual reports are audited by an external auditor.

## 7. Procurement Coordination

Purchases above $5,000 require coordination with Procurement for vendor selection and contract negotiation. Finance approves the final budget.
