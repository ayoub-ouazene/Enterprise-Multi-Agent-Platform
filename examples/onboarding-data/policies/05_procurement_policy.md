# Northstar Labs — Procurement Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Department scope:** Procurement
**Access scope:** All authenticated users

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs supplier selection, purchasing processes, and vendor management.

## 2. Supplier Selection

### 2.1 Evaluation Criteria

Suppliers are evaluated on:

1. **Price** — competitive pricing relative to market.
2. **Quality** — product or service quality and certifications.
3. **Reliability** — on-time delivery rate (minimum 95%).
4. **Compliance** — adherence to legal, environmental, and ethical standards.
5. **Support** — post-sale support and warranty terms.

### 2.2 Minimum Vendors

- For purchases above $10,000, at least 3 vendor quotes are required.
- For purchases above $50,000, a formal RFP (Request for Proposal) is required.

## 3. Purchasing Process

### 3.1 Purchase Requisition

1. Requesting department submits a purchase requisition through the platform.
2. Procurement reviews the requisition for completeness.
3. Procurement identifies and evaluates suppliers.
4. Finance confirms budget availability.
5. Department manager approves the purchase.
6. Procurement issues the purchase order.

### 3.2 Approval Thresholds

| Amount | Approver |
|--------|----------|
| Up to $1,000 | Procurement Specialist |
| $1,000 – $10,000 | Procurement Director |
| $10,000 – $50,000 | Procurement Director + Finance |
| Above $50,000 | Executive leadership |

## 4. Vendor Management

### 4.1 Onboarding Vendors

New vendors complete the following:

- Submit company registration and tax documents.
- Sign a master services agreement (MSA).
- Provide references (minimum 2).
- Pass a compliance and security review.

### 4.2 Vendor Performance Review

- Vendors are reviewed annually.
- Performance metrics include on-time delivery, quality, and cost.
- Underperforming vendors receive a 90-day improvement plan.

## 5. Contract Management

- All contracts are reviewed by Procurement and Finance.
- Contracts above $50,000 require legal review.
- Contracts are stored in the contract repository.
- Renewal notices are sent 60 days before contract expiration.

## 6. Ethical Sourcing

Northstar Labs is committed to ethical sourcing. Suppliers must:

- Comply with labor laws and prohibit child labor.
- Follow environmental regulations.
- Not engage in bribery or corruption.
- Maintain safe working conditions.

## 7. Emergency Purchases

Emergency purchases may bypass the standard process with Procurement Director approval. A post-purchase review is conducted within 5 business days.
